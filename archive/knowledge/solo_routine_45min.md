# Solo tango routine — 45 min (leader, intermediate)

**Focus:** active topics — walk, dissociation, musicality/phrasing — on a fundamentals base.
**Need:** a wall, ~2×2 m of floor, a metronome at **58 bpm** (2 beats/bar), one tango track for the phrasing block. Optional: a stick (broom handle) for posture.
**Built from:** `topic_library.json` + the "Tango Technique At Home" playlist (drill sources tagged `[vNN]`).

> **The bar is "I did something."** If you're tired or short on time, do just blocks 1 + 3 (≈20 min) and call it a win. Quality slow reps beat rushed ones — never sacrifice the slow version to get to the fast one.

---

## 1 · Warm-up — 7 min
*Loosen the chain you actually use; the same warm-up the series uses every time.*

- Shoulder circles ×8 · arm circles ×8 (elbows straight, hands behind on the open)
- Torso side-stretch, hand slides past the knee, hold 3s — 4× each side
- Standing torso rotation (hips/head still, move from the back) — 8× each side
- Forward fold + 3 bounces, roll up head-last — ×8
- Hip circles — 8× each direction
- Calf raises: bounce ×3 → rise to toes, hold 3s, **lower slowly** — ×8

## 2 · Posture & balance prime — 5 min
*Set the axis everything else stands on.* `[v01/v31/v32]`

- **Ribcage placement** ×4: fingers crossed, palms down, arms overhead, "push the ceiling" → ribcage up, weight to front of feet → lower arms, **keep the torso there**.
- **One-leg balance**, 30s each side, gaze fixed on a point on the horizon. Add: knee up → extend leg back to toe → lower on both legs → return (×5/side).

## 3 · The walk — 12 min  ⟵ *core block, active topic*
*Projection first (intention), pendulum second (the dynamic, relaxed quality).* `[v27/v33/v05]`

- **Projection to the side**, metronome, 16× each leg: open free leg + bend standing leg → close + stretch. Free leg **always moving**, foot grazing the floor.
- **Projection forward/back**, 16× each: front foot in *knife* (outside edge), back foot in *toes* (heel kept inside). Shift axis slightly onto the free foot — feel the "intention of going."
- **Pendulum walk** across the room, 4 lengths: swing the free leg from the hip with **minimum force**, let weight + rebound carry it. Notice it's slow at the ends, fast through the middle — that's the dynamic you want walking socially.

## 4 · Dissociation — 7 min  ⟵ *active topic*
*The engine of clear ochos/giros. Keep the torsion LOW, just above the hip.* `[v25/v01/v27]`

- **Standing dissociation** ×16: feet planted, rotate the ribcage over still hips, full range, slow.
- **Walk with dissociation**: upper body faces one wall while feet track the walk — 3 lengths each side. Rule: wherever the torso turns, the free leg keeps its line.
- **Ocho marking** from dissociation + weight change only, no arms — 8× each side. (Bridges into your `ochos` topic when you're ready.)

## 5 · Musicality — phrasing — 11 min  ⟵ *active topic, put the music on*
*This is where technique becomes dancing. Run it through your own CPE-8 + ROLA system — `knowledge/musicality.md`.*

- **Listen first, 1 song, no moving** (~2 min): count the ~8-count phrases, mark where the music *breathes*; name a ROLA for each section (prezencja / napęd / szept…).
- **Quality caminata** (~6 min): start in the **Neutral** preset, then change **one CPE-8 knob per phrase** — 1 min Energia (light↔heavy), 1 min Czas (1-beat↔2-beat transfer), 1 min Rozmiar (mini↔medium), then mix at one-change-per-phrase. Pause where the music breathes (slow transfer through it, don't freeze). `[musicality.md]`
- **Domykanie fraz** (~3 min): put a clean "full stop" (collect, axis, micro-pause) at the end of every other phrase. One tool, done cleanly. `[musicality.md]`

## 6 · Integration / free dance — 3 min
*End on something that feels good — that's what brings you back.*

- Free-dance to one more track, no rules. Let the warm-up/walk/dissociation/phrasing just show up however it shows up. No corrections.

---

**Time:** 7 + 5 + 12 + 7 + 11 + 3 = **45 min**
**Log after:** note in the session file what felt different (a cleaner pivot, a phrase you actually caught) and your energy — that's the signal the coach tracks.
