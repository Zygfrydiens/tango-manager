really does carry the quality change. **But:** some known steps refuse to work in certain gears.

the figure. Forcing it through the wrong gear is where the lead goes muddy (arm-compensation).

## 2026-06-28 · Embrace = gear lever, not a knob

**The idea:** change the embrace *during* a song to shift "gears" — a deliberate management tool for
musicality, not just a feeling.

**Why it works:** an embrace change isn't one CPE-8 knob — it's a *whole preset*. Opening the embrace
pre-loads bigger / more circular / more playful / more spectacular vocabulary all at once; closing it
pre-loads small / intimate / grounded / social. So instead of consciously juggling 8 knobs, Patryk pulls
**one physical lever** and a whole quality gear comes with it.

**How to use it (the discipline):**
- Treat the embrace change as a **section-boundary gear shift** — the physical handle for a ROLA role
  change. That's exactly where CPE-8 says to spend the big contrast.
- Bind 2–3 embrace states to presets for a song: *close* = intimate/small/grounded, *open* =
  playful/big/circular, optional *V-embrace* in between.
- Let the embrace **carry** the change so the conscious mind is freed.

**Watch out:** it's a *boundary* tool, not a *texture* tool. Changing embrace mid-section or per phrase
blows the "one change per phrase, max two per section" readability budget and feels fidgety to the
follower. Mnemonic: **embrace = gear, not knob — shift at the boundary, not in the bar.**

**Seen in the wild:** Brenno Marques & Fátima Caracoche, *La Clavada* (MSTF 2025 Poreč), Section 4 — the
embrace opened and bigger/circular/playful/boleos arrived as a bundle.

**Promote-to-graph candidate:** a `way-of-thinking` concept (`con-embrace-as-gear`), linked to
`mus-presets`, `mus-rola`, `con-section-arc`.
